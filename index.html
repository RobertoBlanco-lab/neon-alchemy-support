Neon Alchemy Support

Drag · Fuse · Evolve

Neon Alchemy is a puzzle game where you drag pieces across the board, fuse symbols, evolve elements, and complete each level by leaving only the required final piece.

Gameplay Help

The goal of each level is shown before and during the game. Drag pieces through the board and combine them to evolve or transform them. Some levels require planning, but the game is designed to remain playable and flexible.
